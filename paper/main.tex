\begin{abstract}
Variational quantum algorithms are limited by non-convex optimization on noisy intermediate-scale hardware. Local optimizers stall on barren plateaus and on rough landscapes produced by physical noise, while calling a language model at every iteration is infeasible because of the input-output latency tax of hosted inference. This work studies a hybrid control architecture in which a language model supervises a fast local optimizer. Every $N_w$ iterations the language model reads a telemetry window of the training run, reports a diagnostic label, justifies it and selects one macroscopic intervention. The intervention is limited to a scaling of the learning rate, a Gaussian perturbation of the variational angles and a restart, drawn from the same action grid given to the classical controllers. The study quantifies the effect of the controller on the final energy gap and separates it from the effect of perturbation alone with random, rule-based, learned and offline contextual-bandit classical controllers. Diagnosis accuracy is measured against simulator ground truth, the fidelity of the stated expected effect is measured from the energy at the decision point against majority-sign and permutation nulls, and the latency budget of the slow loop is reported as a distribution with failed attempts included. Experiments are carried out on Heisenberg, transverse-field Ising and XY models with two, four, eight and sixteen qubits under depolarizing noise of zero, two and five percent, using Qiskit with exact statevector, density-matrix and trajectory-sampled simulator backends.
\end{abstract}

\section{Method}
\subsection{Fast-slow loop}
% Algorithm 1: SPSA for N_w steps, query the controller, apply the action.
\input{fig_methodology.tex}
\begin{algorithm}
\caption{SPSA with a slow-loop controller}\label{alg:fastslow}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require initial angles $\vec{\theta}_0$, budget $T$, window $N_w$, controller $\mathcal{C}$
\State $\eta \gets 1$
\For{$k = 0$ \textbf{to} $T-1$}
  \If{$k \bmod N_w = 0$ \textbf{and} $k > 0$}
    \State $T_k \gets$ telemetry window of the last $N_w$ steps
    \State $a \gets \mathcal{C}(T_k)$
    \State $\vec{\theta}_k \gets \mathrm{intervene}(\vec{\theta}_k, a)$ and update $\eta$
  \EndIf
  \State $\vec{\theta}_{k+1} \gets \mathrm{SPSA\text{-}step}(\vec{\theta}_k, \eta)$
\EndFor
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
